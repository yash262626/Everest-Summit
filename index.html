Sagarmatha. Chomolungma. Everest.

Three names for one mountain, rising from the Mahalangur Himal on the border of Nepal and Tibet — the highest point on Earth above sea level.

TheMountain

Its summit is marine limestone, laid down on the floor of an ancient ocean more than 400 million years ago.

When India began colliding with Asia some 50 million years ago, that seabed was driven nearly nine kilometres into the sky.

The collision has not stopped. Neither has the mountain.
